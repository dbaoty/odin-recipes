Ingredients

for the meat sauce:

  • 3/4 lbs. italian sausage (casing removed)
  • 3/4 lbs. lean ground beef
  • 1 medium onion (diced)
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 can tomato paste (6 oz.)
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes (28 oz.)
  • 1 can tomato sauce (15 oz.)
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon dried Italian seasoning
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 3/4 teaspoon ground black pepper

for the cheese filling:

  • 1 container ricotta cheese (15 oz.)
  • 1 large egg (beaten)
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper

for the assembly:

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups sliced or shredded mozzarella
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan

Instructions

  1. preheat oven to 375°

for the meat sauce:

  1. heat large dutch oven over medium-high heat. add sausage and beef and cook, breaking up chunks and stirring occasionally until browned (6 to 8 minutes)
  2. add onion and garlic, cook until tender (about 5 minutes). add tomato paste, stirring constantly for 1 minute. stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, sugar, Italian seasoning, salt, basil and pepper. bring to a simmer.
  3. reduce heat to medium-low, stirring occasionally until slightly thickened (15-20 minutes)

for the cheese filling:

  1. in a medium bowl, stir together ricotta cheese, egg, parsley, salt and pepper. set aside.

for the assembly:

  1. while sauce is simmering, bring large pot of salted water to a boil over high heat. add lasagna noodles and cook al dente. drain, rinse with cold water and drain again.
  2. lightly grease 9x13 in. casserole dish
  3. spread 2 cups of meat sauce in bottom of prepared dish. layer 4 cooked noodles in an even layer. spread half of cheese filling in an even layer over noodles. repeat.
  4. top final layer with mozzarella and parmesan. cover with foil
  5. bake 30 minutes. uncover and continue baking until cheese is melted and lasagna is heated through (about 15 minutes). optionally, broil cheese until browned. let rest 15 minutes before serving.
