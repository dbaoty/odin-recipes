Fried Egg

Description

So good, even chickens eat them

- ???

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 egg

Instructions

  1. add 2 tbsp. olive oil in pan over medium heat
  2. crack egg into pan
  3. allow egg to cook, gently tipping pan to redistribute oil until egg edges are crispy and yolk is cooked as desired.
  4. transfer egg to plate
